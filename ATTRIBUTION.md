## What is derived

`backend/data/catalogue.jsonl.gz` is a sample of that dataset, selected and
transformed by `backend/scripts/ingest.py`. It retains the source `asin`,
`title`, `price`, `stars`, `reviews`, `categoryName`, and image and product URLs.
It is a Derivative Database under ODC-By §1.0 and is offered under the same
licence.

Descriptions, semantic attributes, English titles and embeddings are generated by
this project and are not part of the source dataset.

`backend/data/mock/` is different in kind: those products are **invented**, with
fabricated ASINs, and contain no data from the source dataset at all. Nothing in
this section applies to them.

## What the licence does not cover

**ODC-By §2.4 is the important limit.** The licence governs rights in the
*Database* — its structure, selection and arrangement — and expressly does not
cover copyright or other rights in the individual Contents. Product titles and
images are Amazon's and their respective rights holders', not the dataset
author's, and the ODC-By grant says nothing about them.

Mitigations applied regardless of the licence, for that reason:

- **No image files are stored or redistributed.** Products reference their
  original Amazon CDN URLs, so images are served by Amazon, not by this project.
- **Attribution to the dataset and to Amazon as the underlying source**, in the
  application and in this file.
- **Ingest scripts are committed**, so every transformation from source row to
  shipped record is auditable rather than asserted.
- **The sample is a demonstration**, not a substitute for the source dataset.

Product titles and identifiers remain the property of their respective rights
holders.
